Chapter I

The World of Westeros

Westeros is a long, restless continent. Snow and ancient forests lie in the north, green farmland in the middle, deserts and warm seas in the far south.

For thousands of years it was a patchwork of kingdoms at war. Then Aegon Targaryen arrived on dragonback and bound most of them under one crown. About three hundred years later, the story begins.

Seven great regions still follow the Iron Throne in King's Landing, but loyalty is only as strong as the king who holds it. When one ruler dies, every lord begins to count his swords.

Chapter III

The Great Houses

Every house has a banner, a seat of power and a motto it will defend with its life. Six of them shaped the story more than any others.

Winterfell · The North · Sigil: a grey direwolf

The Wardens of the North have ruled from Winterfell for thousands of years. They are honorable, stubborn and used to the cold, and they are always the first to face whatever comes from beyond the Wall.

House Lannister

Casterly Rock · The Westerlands · Sigil: a golden lion

The richest house in the realm, sitting on mountains of gold. Wealth and patience have made the Lannisters kingmakers, and they are ready to use both whenever the crown is within reach.

House Targaryen

Dragonstone · Sigil: a three-headed red dragon

A family from old Valyria, one of the few that escaped the Doom that destroyed it. They tamed dragons, took the Seven Kingdoms and ruled for nearly three centuries, until Robert's Rebellion ended their reign.

Chapter IV

The Iron Throne

Legend says the Iron Throne was forged from the swords of Aegon's defeated enemies, melted together by the breath of his dragon, Balerion.

The blades are twisted and sharp on purpose. Aegon wanted no king to sit comfortably.

Many have wanted the chair. Aerys Targaryen, the Mad King, was overthrown. Robert Baratheon took it after a rebellion. Joffrey, Tommen and Cersei held it after him, each one ruling through fear.

In the end it was Daenerys Targaryen who reached King's Landing with an army and her last dragon. Her fire took the city, and then Drogon melted the Throne itself.

The chair was gone, but the struggle over who would rule was not.

Chapter V

The War of Five Kings

When King Robert died, the realm split. Joffrey, Robb Stark, Renly Baratheon, Stannis Baratheon and Balon Greyjoy each claimed a crown. This is how the war went.

  1. The spark

    A king dies, a boy takes his place

    After Robert's death, Joffrey is crowned. He has Eddard Stark arrested for treason and beheaded in front of the Great Sept of Baelor.

  2. Five crowns

    The North will not kneel

    Robb Stark's bannermen declare him King in the North. Renly and Stannis each claim the Iron Throne, and Balon Greyjoy declares the Iron Islands a kingdom again.

  3. First blood

    The Whispering Wood

    Robb outmaneuvers a Lannister army at night and wins a great victory. Jaime Lannister is taken prisoner.

  4. A shadow in the tent

    Renly is murdered

    Renly Baratheon is killed in his own camp by a shadow, born from the magic of Melisandre. His army scatters, and the Tyrells soon ally with the Lannisters.

  5. Green fire

    The Battle of the Blackwater

    Stannis attacks King's Landing by sea. Tyrion's wildfire burns his fleet, and Tywin Lannister arrives with the Tyrell army to break the siege.

  6. The cruelest night

    The Red Wedding

    At the Twins, Walder Frey and Roose Bolton betray the Starks. Robb, his mother Catelyn and much of the northern army are slaughtered at a wedding feast. The war in the North ends there.

Chapter VI

Fire Made Flesh

Dragons had been dead for generations, and most people thought they were only stories. Then Daenerys Targaryen stepped into Khal Drogo's funeral pyre and walked out alive, with three newborn dragons on her shoulders.

She named them after the men she had lost. Drogon for her husband, Rhaegal for her brother Rhaegar, and Viserion for her brother Viserys.

  • Drogon The largest and most fierce. He survived the war and carried Daenerys away at its end.
  • Rhaegal Shot down by Euron Greyjoy's fleet near Dragonstone.
  • Viserion Killed by the Night King beyond the Wall, and raised again as an undead dragon with blue eyes.

Three centuries earlier, Aegon the Conqueror had flown on Balerion the Black Dread, the largest dragon the world had ever known. A single dragon was once enough to change the fate of a continent.

Chapter VII

The Night King

Beyond the Wall, the cold is older than any kingdom, and something moves in it.

The White Walkers are an ancient race of ice. Thousands of years ago, the Children of the Forest created the first of them to fight the invading First Men. The experiment went wrong, and their weapon turned on everyone.

Their leader is the Night King. Every person he kills can be raised as a wight, a corpse that obeys him. The dead do not tire and do not fear, and they are only ever on his side.

For centuries the Night's Watch has guarded the Wall against them, but many in the south believe the threat is just a story. By the time the realm took notice, the dead were already marching.

Chapter VIII

The Long Night

The Battle of Winterfell

The living made their stand at Winterfell, with Starks, Targaryens, Dothraki, Unsullied and the Knights of the Vale all fighting behind the same walls.

The Dothraki charged into the dark with flaming swords, and the light vanished one blade at a time. The dead overran the castle's defences, and the living were forced back into the keep.

Theon Greyjoy died protecting Bran in the godswood. In the end Arya Stark slipped through the chaos and drove a Valyrian steel dagger into the Night King. He shattered, and every one of his dead fell with him.

Chapter IX

The Game of Thrones

Every crown has a cost.

When the war was done, the great lords gathered and chose Bran Stark as king of the Six Kingdoms. Sansa Stark was crowned Queen in the North, and Westeros was divided again, this time by agreement.

Jon Snow went back to the far north, beyond the Wall, and the long game of thrones was finally over.
